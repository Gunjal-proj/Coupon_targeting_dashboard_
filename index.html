Your campaign plan

How the profit is calculated

Profit = expected redeemers × (new-sale share × extra profit − discount) − households mailed × cost to send.

Expected redeemers = households mailed × redemption rate × the share of redeemers each approach reaches at that depth, taken from the test campaigns.

Recommendations

What I would tell the marketing team

  1. Rank households before every mailing

    Mailing the model's top 30% reaches of redeemers for 30% of the postage. The simulator shows where the profit peaks for your costs.

  2. Start with the simple rule

    "Send it to people who already buy these products with coupons" reaches on its own and runs in any CRM. The model adds about points on top.

  3. Personalise the offer

    Campaigns that chose coupons from each household's history redeemed at the rate of mass campaigns.

  4. Prove the extra sales with a holdout

    Keep a random 10% of each targeted list back and compare their spend with those who got the coupon. of redemptions went on products households already bought, so this test decides whether the campaign pays.

Method

How this was built

Data

84.51° Complete Journey: households, purchase records from to , campaigns and coupon redemptions. Released into the public domain (CC0) and packaged in the completejourney R package.

Approach, adapted from the AmExpert 2019 winners

  • One row per household and campaign. The outcome is whether the household redeemed at least one of that campaign's coupons.
  • Every signal uses only purchases and redemptions from before the campaign started, the rule all five winners stressed.
  • Signals cover overlap between the campaign's coupon products and past purchases, past redemptions, coupon and promotion habits, spend and trips, and demographics where available.
  • The simple rule copies the Rank 2 winner's no-model ranking: products bought with a coupon, then products bought at all, then past redemption rate.
  • Test campaigns started on or after . Training used only campaigns that had ended by then. Intervals come from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of households within campaigns.

Read the winners' write-up on Analytics Vidhya and their code on GitHub.

Model comparison

AUC within campaign. The final model was picked on the training campaigns before looking at the test.

Limits

  • For personalised campaigns the data does not say which 16 coupons each household got, so the model works at campaign level.
  • A redemption is not proof of an extra sale. The simulator's new-sale share is an assumption.
  • The test period has campaigns, and one large personalised campaign holds most of its redeemers.
  • US grocery data from 2017. Response rates in other markets and years will differ.
