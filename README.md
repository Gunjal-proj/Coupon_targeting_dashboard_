# Who Should Get the Coupon?

**Live dashboard:** https://YOUR-PROJECT-NAME.netlify.app  <!-- EDIT: paste your Netlify link here -->

A coupon targeting and ROI dashboard built on 84.51°'s Complete Journey retail data
(2,469 households, 1.47 million purchases, 27 coupon campaigns). It applies the approach that won the
**AmExpert 2019 hackathon** (American Express × Analytics Vidhya), where the task was to predict which
customers would redeem which coupons.

## The business problem

A grocery retailer mails coupon campaigns to its shoppers, and most of that mail is wasted:
**88% of household mailings led to no redemption at all.** Each unused mailer costs money, and some
coupons go to people who would have bought the product anyway. The marketing team needs to know
**which households each campaign should go to**, how deep to mail, and whether the coupons create
extra sales.

## What I built

1. **A campaign tracker** of all 27 campaigns: audience, timing, redemption rate, personalised vs mass.
2. **Redemption drivers**: past coupon behaviour and overlap with what a household already buys
   predict redemption far better than demographics.
3. **A targeting model** trained on earlier campaigns and tested on later ones it never saw,
   compared with a simple rule (the AmExpert Rank 2 winner's no-model ranking) and random mailing.
4. **An ROI simulator**: set the cost per mailer, discount, profit per new sale and the share of
   redemptions that are truly new, and see the most profitable mailing depth.
5. **An incrementality check**: how many redeemed coupons were for products the household was
   already buying, and the holdout test that would settle it.

## How it works

- **One row per household × campaign.** Outcome: did the household redeem at least one coupon from
  that campaign?
- **No peeking at the future.** Every feature uses only purchases and redemptions from before the
  campaign started, the rule all five AmExpert winners stressed.
- **Time-based test.** Train on campaigns that ended before October 2017; test on the 8 campaigns
  that started after. Households are ranked within each campaign, the way a marketer would use the
  scores.
- **Models:** simple rule, logistic regression and LightGBM. The final model was chosen by
  cross-validation on the training campaigns, before looking at the test set. 95% intervals come
  from 1,000 bootstrap resamples.

## Run it yourself

```bash

python run_pipeline.py      # about two minutes; downloads the data on first run
# then open site/index.html in a browser
```

## Limitations

- For personalised campaigns the data does not record which 16 coupons each household received,
  so the model works at campaign level rather than coupon level.
- A redemption is not proof of an extra sale; the simulator's new-sale share is an assumption that
  only a holdout test can measure.
- The test period has 8 campaigns, and one large personalised campaign holds most of its redeemers.
- US grocery data from 2017.
